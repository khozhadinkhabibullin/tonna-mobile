# Attribution

These two image files are local assets used by the interface:

- `profile-portrait.webp` — source: [Unsplash photo 1500648767791-00dcc994a43e](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1500648767791-00dcc994a43e). Resized and cropped for the profile image.
- `vehicle-truck.webp` — source: [Unsplash photo 1601584115197-04ecc0da31d7](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1601584115197-04ecc0da31d7). Resized and cropped for the carrier vehicle image.

The truck image is used only as transport imagery, not as a cargo photograph. No cargo photographs are bundled. The cargo-photo section starts empty and accepts multiple images chosen by the user. The app loads these files locally and does not make runtime requests to the source URLs.
